FAQ

Common Questions from Overseas Buyers

What files should I send for a quotation?

Please send 2D drawings (PDF/DWG/DXF) and 3D models (STEP/IGES/X_T) if available. Also include material, quantity, tolerance and surface finish requirements.

Can you machine very large parts?

Yes. We specialize in large-scale machining, including floor-type boring & milling. Please send the part size and weight so we can confirm the suitable machine.

Do you provide welding and post-weld machining?

Yes. We can weld heavy structures and then machine critical surfaces to meet your dimensional requirements.

How fast can I get a quote?

Usually within 24–48 hours after we receive complete drawings and requirements. Complex projects may take longer.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. We respect customer intellectual property and can sign an NDA before receiving your drawings.
